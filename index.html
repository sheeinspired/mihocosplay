About Miho

A passion for transforming imagination into reality.

Miho is a cosplayer from the Philippines who has been bringing beloved anime and game characters to life since 2012. What started as a passion for anime and games grew into a career of competition, performance, guesting, judging, and international representation.

Her journey has taken her from local Philippine competitions to the World Cosplay Summit in Japan and international events across Asia and the Middle East. Along the way, she has become a champion, ambassador, guest, judge, speaker, and creative partner.

Her Journey

From local competitions to international stages.

From her first competitions in the Philippines to representing her country on the World Cosplay Summit stage, Miho’s journey reflects more than a decade of growth in cosplay. Her experiences now span championships, ambassador roles, guest appearances, judging, partnerships, and international conventions.
